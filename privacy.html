Tera — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when using Tera, an AI-powered virtual streamer and virtual avatar application.

1. Information Tera May Process

Depending on the enabled features and integrations, Tera may process information such as conversation messages, display names, platform identifiers, livestream interaction events, supporter events, images submitted for vision features, and contextual memory information.

2. Conversation and Memory

Tera may store selected conversation context and memory to provide continuity between interactions. Not every message or viewer is necessarily stored as long-term memory.

3. Livestream Data

When livestream features are enabled, Tera may process chat messages, supporter events, gift or reward events, and other livestream information required to provide interactive behavior.

4. Discord and Other Platforms

When third-party integrations are enabled, Tera may process information provided by those platforms to deliver requested functionality.

5. Images and Vision Features

Images submitted to Tera for supported vision features may be processed by the configured AI vision service in order to generate a response.

6. Data Storage

Depending on the configured Tera features, information may be stored locally by the Tera application for memory, conversation continuity, livestream history, and related functionality.

7. Third-Party Services

Tera may rely on third-party services for functions such as AI model inference, livestream integrations, voice generation, avatar control, or communication services. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect locally stored application data and credentials. Users should not expose API keys, access tokens, passwords, or other secrets through public repositories or public websites.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Stored information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide memory, personalization, troubleshooting, or livestream-related features. Retention and deletion behavior may vary depending on the feature and storage configuration.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Tera and its integrations evolve. The latest version will be published on this website.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use the contact information provided by the Tera project owner.
